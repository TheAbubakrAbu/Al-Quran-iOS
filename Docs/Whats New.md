# Version 2.6.5

Test Mode and a Revision plan for memorizing, Word Study for every word, Simple Mode, and recitation that plays as far as you want.

Memorize:
- Test Mode hides the text until you tap, so you can recite from memory and check each ayah.
- The Quran Planner adds a Revision plan: a juz range or chosen surahs, revised every few days, with one reminder for both plans.

Al-Quran:
- Word Study: double tap any word for its meaning, grammar, root, themes, tajweed and qiraat.
- Play From Ayah now plays to the end of the page, surah, juz, or the whole Quran.
- Repeat Forever for an ayah, a surah or a custom range, downloaded once and replayed offline.
- Two mushaf pages side by side in landscape, and both readers use the full width.
- Tajweed colors the heavy and light laam in the name of Allah, and you can pick your own color for every rule.
- A rebuilt Qiraat Explorer: see every riwayah at once, or compare two.
- Summarize any ayah with Apple Intelligence.
- The screen stays awake while you follow the highlighted ayah.

Widgets:
- Chosen Ayah widget: pin any ayah or bookmark to your Home or Lock Screen.
- The Last Listened widget gets a Play button.

Islamic Tools:
- Today: the ayah, word, dua and Name of the day on one screen.
- A Reading Test with 21 levels, from single letters to text without vowels.
- Tajweed Foundations is now a 58-lesson course with quizzes and Quran examples.
- New libraries: Proving Islam, Miracles of the Prophets, and Prophecies of the Prophet.
- Ask AI cites its sources with numbered cards you can open.
- Two new wallpapers: OC Ummah's desert night, and a Free Palestine poster with the Dome of the Rock.

Settings:
- Simple Mode keeps only the essentials on screen. Switch to Full any time.
- A search bar on every settings page, and 150 Tips & Tricks.

Bug fixes and optimizations:
- Faster search, smoother playback, and dozens of crash fixes across iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

# Version 2.6.4

A daily reminder card, a journal, the 99 Names in depth, the miracles of the Quran, and Sunnah reminders rooted in hadith.

Reminder of the Day:
- One card a day at the top of the Islam tab: a verse, a hadith, a sunnah, a dua, a dhikr, or a Name of Allah.
- It opens once when you first open the app that day, and you can keep any card as a saved reflection.
- New Ayah of the Day, Reminder of the Day and Name of Allah widgets for the home and lock screens.

Journal:
- Write what you are sitting with, tag it, and find it again by tag or by search.
- Attach an ayah by reference, a bookmarked hadith, or a dua from the app's collections.
- Notes you left on bookmarked ayahs and hadiths gather here as margin notes.

Sunnah Reminders:
- Al-Kahf on Friday, al-Mulk before sleep, the Mu'awwidhat, and more, each at the time you choose.
- Every reminder cites the hadith it is rooted in, and opening it takes you to the surah.
- Reminders for your last-read position, your reading streak, and a daily dua, each with its own switch.

Al-Quran:
- Word of the Day: one Quranic word a day, with its root and where it appears.
- Similar Ayahs and Mutashabihat: the verses that echo each other, with the shared words tinted.
- Morphology for any word, and a Qiraat Explorer that walks the places the readings differ, with audio.
- Browse by theme from four corpora, and light a theme up so it stays marked as you read.
- Tap a word twice for its meaning, and search inside the word-by-word layout.

Islamic Tools:
- The 99 Names in depth: what each name means, what living by it asks, and every verse that names it.
- Miracles of the Quran: a library of articles on what the Quran said before it could be known.
- Hisn al-Muslim, the fortress of the Muslim, with audio for every dua.
- Search the whole Islam tab at once.

Your Progress:
- A daily activity dashboard: reading, listening and dhikr as rings, with a streak.

Bug fixes and optimizations throughout.

Ask AI: a real conversation about the Quran, two new Arabic faces, tajweed on every riwayah comparison, and a much deeper Islam tab.

Ask AI:

- Reading plan: an optional reminder the day before.

Islamic Tools:

- Aqeedah: Tawhid, Shirk, Kufr, Bid'ah, the Mawlid, Salafiyyah, Quran and Sunnah, and the schools of creed.
- Scholars: the scholars among the Sahabah, the Salaf and the imams, at-Tabari, Ibn Taymiyyah, Ibn al-Qayyim, adh-Dhahabi, Ibn Kathir, and those who followed.
- Answering: Sufism, the Shia, Christianity, Judaism, Hinduism, Paganism, Buddhism, and Atheism.

Smaller and faster:
- The bundled Quran data was repacked: a smaller download that opens quicker.

Bug fixes and optimizations throughout.

# Version 2.6.0

On-device AI search, a Quran planner, tafsir in Arabic and English, a real mushaf, and everything a beginner needs to start reading.

AI Search:
- Search the Quran by meaning, not just keywords: "patience in hardship" finds ayahs about sabr even when neither word appears. Built with Apple's on-device language frameworks: private, offline, and free.
- On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Ask AI answers your question in a few sentences drawn only from the ayahs it cites right below the answer, and it never gives religious rulings.

Quran Planner:
- Pick a finish date, or a daily pace, and get a daily amount that adjusts itself when you miss a day.
- Streaks, a weekly chart, and a running average show the pace you are really keeping.
- Progress is your khatm itself, so reading in the app advances the plan without any check in.

Al-Quran:
- Tafsir for any ayah: Ibn Kathir, Maarif Ul Quran, and Tazkirul Quran in English, plus Ibn Kathir, al-Tabari, and as-Sa'di in Arabic, saved for offline reading, with an option to download them all.
- A faster, retypeset mushaf: pages open and flip quicker, every line fills the full width, and spacing is even in Arabic and English alike.
- Compare the qiraat and English translations side by side, right from any ayah.
- Search the whole Quran from wherever you are reading, and use Siri too: play any surah, play a random surah, or resume your last listen, in English and Arabic.
- Offline ayah playback in the reciter's own voice: for a dozen reciters, downloading their surahs also fetches precise ayah timings, so single ayahs and ranges play from the file.
- Select multiple ayahs at once to share, copy, bookmark, note, or switch to beginner mode together.

Apple Watch:
- Full-surah recitation now plays on the watch; connect AirPods (or pick a route when asked) and every reciter works in their own voice.
- Settings now sync more reliably between iPhone and Apple Watch, including right after pairing a new watch.

Islamic Tools:
- New Pillars & Beliefs and How-To Guides: the Five Pillars, the Six Pillars of Faith, the Prophet Muhammad (pbuh), and step-by-step guides for everyday worship, written for converts and beginners.
- Tajweed Foundations explains the rules behind the colors before you ever open a mushaf.
- Eight new dua collections, each supplication authenticated with its exact source, and Listen buttons that read the Arabic aloud across duas, adhkar, and the Arabic alphabet.
- Settings reorganized with a search that jumps straight to any setting.

Bug fixes and optimizations:
- Performance, reliability, and polish throughout.
