## Overview
Al-Quran is a simply designed and feature-rich app that helps Muslims read, understand, and live with the Quran. Whether you're a lifelong believer, a new convert, or simply learning to read Arabic for the first time, this app provides everything you need - from the complete Quran, tafsir, and over 60 reciters to on-device AI search, a reading planner, and essential Islamic tools.

## Unique Features
- **On-Device AI Search** – Search the Quran by meaning, not just keywords - "patience in hardship" finds ayahs about sabr even when neither word appears. Built entirely with Apple's on-device language frameworks: private, offline, and free. On devices with Apple Intelligence, **Ask AI** answers questions in a few sentences drawn only from the ayahs it cites right below the answer - and it never gives religious rulings.
- **Arabic Beginner Mode** – Spaces out Arabic letters to help beginners recognize them more easily while learning to read the Quran.
- **Unique Ayah Sharing** – Share Quranic verses as text or images effortlessly, or select multiple ayahs at once to share, copy, bookmark, or annotate together.
- **Quran Planner** – Pick a finish date (or a daily pace) and get a daily reading amount that automatically redistributes when you miss a day, with streaks and a weekly chart - built on khatm progress, so reading in the app advances the plan by itself.
- **Siri Shortcuts** – Play any surah by name or number, play a random surah, or resume your last listened surah - in English and Arabic.

## The Complete Quran
- View by Surah or Juz, with Juz displaying the starting ayah.
- Read as a scrolling ayah list or as a mushaf, page by page.
- Read in Arabic, English transliteration, and translation (Saheeh International and Clear Quran).
- Color-coded tajweed with a built-in reference for the rules.
- Tafsir for any ayah - Ibn Kathir, Maarif Ul Quran, and Tazkirul Quran in English, plus Ibn Kathir, al-Tabari, and as-Sa'di in Arabic - fetched once and saved for offline reading, with an option to download all tafsirs.
- Compare the qiraat (readings) and English translations side by side.
- Select multiple ayahs to share, copy, bookmark, note, or switch to beginner mode all at once.
- Bookmark, favorite, add notes, and track last read/listened ayah with recent history.
- Powerful Quran search by keyword, meaning (on-device AI), ayah (e.g., `5:27`), page, or Juz - reachable from anywhere you are reading.
- Remove tashkeel (diacritics) for cleaner reading.
- Listen to recitations from over 60 reciters and qiraat, with full-Surah and verse-by-verse playback, offline downloads, and offline ayah timings for many reciters so single ayahs play in the reciter's own voice.
- Random Surah mode for discovery.
- Widgets for the Quran, including an Ayah of the Day widget and widgets for your last read ayah and last listened surah.

## Essential Islamic Tools
- Common adhkar (remembrances) and duas (supplications), each authenticated with its exact source, with Listen buttons that read the Arabic aloud.
- Tasbih counter for dhikr tracking.
- 99 Names of Allah with meanings and explanations.
- Hijri calendar converter.
- Islamic wallpapers for inspiration.
- How-To Guides for the everyday acts of worship.

## Perfect for Converts and Beginners
- Learn the Five Pillars of Islam and Six Pillars of Faith.
- Understand Islamic beliefs, the Prophet Muhammad (pbuh), and key teachings.
- Tajweed Foundations explains the rules behind the colors before you ever open a mushaf.

## Learn the Arabic Alphabet Easily
- View Arabic letters, special letters, and numbers.
- Learn letter forms, diacritics, and Quranic stopping signs for better recitation.
- Listen buttons read every letter and example aloud.

## Fully Customizable and User-Friendly
- Customize Quran text size, Arabic fonts, accent colors, and reading settings.
- A dedicated Quran settings screen, with a settings search that jumps straight to any option.
- Widgets and Apple Watch support for quick Quran access, including full-surah recitation on the watch.
- Simple and clean interface for effortless navigation.
